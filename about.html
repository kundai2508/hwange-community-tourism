About the Hwange Community

People, culture and land beside Hwange National Park

Our Place

Hwange, in north-western Zimbabwe, is home to Hwange National Park, the country's largest game reserve, known for its large elephant herds. Communities living around the park have long shared the land with wildlife and carry deep knowledge of the bush, farming and local traditions.

Our Story

This portal was created so that local hosts, guides, artisans and cooks can present their own experiences directly to visitors and receive fair income from tourism.

Our Values

Respect

Visitors are guests. We ask them to respect customs, homes and wildlife.

Fair Benefit

Income is shared among hosts, guides and community projects.

Sustainability

Small groups and low-impact activities protect people and nature.
